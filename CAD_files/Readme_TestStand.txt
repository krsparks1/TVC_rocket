For easy PID tuning on the ground, a test stand with dual axis rotation can be helpful. It is made up of an inner circle that clamps around the center of gravity of the rocket and enables motion in the x-axis, and an outer circle which is attached to the inner circle and enables movement in the y-axis. 

--Go to "https://tinyurl.com/muvw57mz" to copy the file from Onshape. 

*important note: All parts in the Workspace should be 3d printed except for the "Stand(wood)", and "Dowels" Parts Studios
